Terms

1. What we sell

CitedBy audits websites. You enter your address; we crawl the pages we can reach, check each one against our list of checks (including how AI search crawlers see your pages) and give you a report with a fix for each issue. Plans: Single audit (€9, one site, up to 500 pages, 30 days of access), Starter (€19 a month), Pro (€49 a month) and Agency (€99 a month), each also yearly at ten times the monthly price. The Fix Pack (€390, paid once) is the done-for-you option: we apply the fixes. There is no free plan. An account is required before an audit runs.

2. What we promise, and what we don't

We promise that the crawl and the report are produced as described and that you can have your money back within 14 days (see Refunds). For the Fix Pack we promise the work and the measurement: changes live, or files delivered, within 7 days of the day you give us access or tell us you want the files, and a recount at day 30 counted the same way as your first audit. Nobody controls what an assistant answers, so we do not promise rankings, mentions or revenue. Answers from AI assistants vary between runs; our counts are a sample, not a guarantee.

Payments are taken by Stripe; we never see your card details. The price shown on the page when you buy is the price you pay. Prices are final prices: any VAT that applies is included and nothing is added at checkout. Businesses can ask for an invoice at hello@citedby.eu. The Single audit and the Fix Pack are single payments and do not renew. Starter, Pro and Agency renew each month (or year) until you cancel from your account page; cancelling stops the next charge and you keep access until the period you paid for ends. The older CitedBy Monthly (€149) is no longer sold; existing subscribers cancel by replying to any report.
