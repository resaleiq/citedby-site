The Search generative AI control rolls out to all sites worldwide

31 Aug 2026 · AI Overviews · AI Mode · Discover · Source Search Console Help: Search generative AI control · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“As of August 31, 2026, we’ve rolled out this control to all websites worldwide.”

Quote (original wording)

What you can do. Open Settings > Search generative AI in Search Console and confirm each property is set to include the site, unless the owner chose otherwise.

What CitedBy checks: Not checked Not checked. The setting lives in Search Console and CitedBy cannot see it.

Generative AI performance reports roll out to all sites worldwide

31 Aug 2026 · AI Overviews · AI Mode · Discover · Source Google Search Central Blog (note added to the 3 June post) · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“As of August 31, 2026, we've rolled out these insights to all websites worldwide.”

Quote (original wording)

What you can do. Check the Generative AI performance report is now visible for every property, in every country you serve.

What CitedBy checks: Not checked Not read. Same as the launch entry: CitedBy does not read this report.

Google launches AI Overviews and AI Mode in France

22 Jul 2026 · AI Overviews · AI Mode · Source Blog Google France · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“La Recherche Google franchit une étape majeure aujourd’hui en France avec le lancement des Aperçus IA (AI Overviews) et du Mode IA (AI Mode).”

Quote (original wording) (Source page and quote are in French.)

What you can do. Read the French AI impressions in the Generative AI performance report and check which French questions now show an AI Overview.

What CitedBy checks: Not checked Not checked. CitedBy does not measure whether a question shows an AI Overview.

Google says llms.txt is not needed for Search

15 Jun 2026 · Google Search · Source Google Search Central, documentation updates · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“while these files aren't needed for Google Search (and won't negatively or positively impact your visibility or rankings)”

Quote (original wording)

What you can do. Keep llms.txt optional: publish it for other tools if wanted, but do not expect Google Search visibility from it.

What CitedBy checks: Checked Checked. A missing llms.txt shows as an optional notice, never an error, because no major assistant is confirmed to read it. Check yours free: llms.txt checker

Google Analytics adds a channel for AI assistant traffic

13 May 2026 · ChatGPT · Gemini · Claude · Copilot · DeepSeek · Grok · Source Google Analytics Help, release notes · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“Google Analytics now provides a dedicated way to measure and analyze traffic originating from popular AI assistants.”

Quote (original wording)

What you can do. Open the Default Channel Group in GA4 reports, check that the AI Assistant channel shows, and add a custom rule only for AI referrers that still land in Referral.

What CitedBy checks: Not checked Not checked. CitedBy reads your pages, not your analytics.

Lighthouse adds an agentic browsing category with an llms.txt audit

7 May 2026 · Lighthouse · Source Lighthouse v13.3.0 release notes (GoogleChrome/lighthouse) · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“New agentic browsing category added to default config”

Quote (original wording)

What you can do. Make /llms.txt answer 200 or 404, never a server error: Lighthouse flags server errors on it and marks a 404 Not Applicable.

What CitedBy checks: Partly checked Partly. Every crawl requests /llms.txt: 200 counts as present, 404 or 410 as missing (an optional notice). CitedBy does not flag a server error on it as an issue. Check yours free: llms.txt checker

11 Mar 2026 · Google Search · Source Google Search Central Blog · Read on 22 Oct 2026

“The branded queries filter is now available to all eligible sites.”

Quote (original wording)

What you can do. In the Search Console Performance report, apply the branded and non-branded filter and read the two groups separately.

What CitedBy checks: Not checked Not checked. It changes how you read Search Console, not how your pages are built.
