What every page is checked for

Five categories of checks, run on every page we crawl. The site audit comes first; AI-search readiness is one category.

  • Links, speed and indexingStatus codes, redirects, broken links, canonicals, sitemap and indexability.
  • Titles, headings and schemaTitles, descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, and Organization, Product and FAQ markup.
  • Readable contentWhether the main copy is in the HTML each page serves, without JavaScript.
  • AI-search readinessrobots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, llms.txt, and bot access past your firewall, so ChatGPT and Perplexity can read you.
  • Answer qualityDoes each page answer its question early, with the specific facts, prices and sizes buyers look for?
  • Ready-to-paste fixesFor every failed check, the exact change for that page, with code you can copy.

Questions

What does the audit read?

The HTML each page serves, plus robots.txt and llms.txt. We do not log in, run JavaScript or get around bot walls. A check we could not read is shown as not measured, never as a pass.

Is there a free trial?

No. Crawling and analysing every page costs money, so every plan is paid. You have 14 days to ask for a refund.

How many pages can you check?

100 on a single audit, up to 10,000 per crawl on Agency.

Can I put my agency's logo on the report?

Yes, on Agency: the PDF carries your name and logo instead of ours.

Do you guarantee citations from ChatGPT?

No. The audit finds what blocks or weakens your pages for AI assistants and tells you how to fix it. It cannot promise what an assistant will say.
