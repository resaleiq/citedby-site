Questions

Why pay for an audit when free checkers exist?

Free checkers test one page, and crawlers you install hand you a list of problems. CitedBy checks every page up to your plan limit and writes the exact fix for each issue, so you paste the fix and skip the research. If it is not worth €9 to you, the 14-day guarantee refunds it.

What is an AI search audit and why do I need one?

An AI search audit checks whether AI assistants and search engines can reach, read and quote your pages: crawler access in robots.txt, readable text without JavaScript, llms.txt, structured data, titles and internal links. You need one because an assistant that cannot read a page cannot cite it, and most blockers are fixed with a few lines.

How do I check if AI assistants can read my website?

Paste your address into the free check on this page, or use the free AI crawler checker at citedby.eu/tools/ai-crawler-check. It shows whether robots.txt and your server let GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Googlebot in, with the exact lines to change.

What is the difference between SEO and AI search readiness?

SEO is about ranking in a list of links. AI search readiness is whether an assistant can fetch your page, read its text without running JavaScript and lift a sentence as an answer. They overlap on crawlability, titles and structured data, and differ on bot access (GPTBot, ClaudeBot) and answer-first writing. More in GEO vs AEO vs SEO.

Can I fix AI crawler access issues myself?

Yes. Most access problems are a few lines in robots.txt or one setting in your host or CDN. Each issue in the audit comes with the exact lines to paste, and our pages for Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, WordPress.com and Cloudflare show where each setting sits.

How do I know if ChatGPT can find my site?

ChatGPT search uses OAI-SearchBot, and ChatGPT-User fetches a page when someone asks about it. If robots.txt or your firewall blocks them, ChatGPT cannot read your pages, and the free AI crawler check tests both. Whether it then names you in an answer is something nobody can guarantee.

What structured data do AI assistants need?

No major assistant publishes a required list. JSON-LD tells them what a page is: Organization on the home page, Product on product pages, Article on posts and FAQPage where a page answers questions. The audit flags pages with none and writes the JSON-LD to paste.

How long does a website audit take?

Our crawler waits at least 1 second between requests to your site, so a 500-page audit takes at least 8 minutes and a larger site takes longer. The report opens in your account when the crawl finishes.
