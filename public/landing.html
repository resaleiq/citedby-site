Questions

Why pay for an audit when free checkers exist?

Free checkers test one page, and crawlers you install hand you a list of problems. CitedBy checks every page up to your plan limit and writes the exact fix for each issue, so you paste the fix and skip the research. If it is not worth €9 to you, the 14-day guarantee refunds it.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Starter and Pro start with 7 days free. You enter a card, we charge €0 today, and the Stripe page shows the date and amount of the first charge before you confirm. The trial covers 1 site and up to 100 pages, with the full report; your plan's full allowance starts with the first payment. We email you 3 days before the first charge with a cancel link. Cancel in your account, and if you cancel before the first charge you pay nothing. One trial per email address and card.

Why do you need my card for a trial?

A crawl costs us real money, and a card keeps the trial to one per person. It is not a trap: the charge date and amount are shown up front, you get a reminder 3 days before, and after the first charge the 14-day money-back guarantee still applies. If you want no subscription at all, the €9 audit has none.

What do I get for €9?

One audit of one site, up to 100 pages: a short list of what to fix first, every issue ranked by severity with the pages it affects, a page-by-page view, a ready-to-paste fix for each issue and a PDF. The report stays in your account for 30 days.

What does the audit read?

The HTML each page serves, plus robots.txt, your sitemap and llms.txt. We read public pages only. We do not log in, and we do not run your JavaScript, so a page that arrives empty until JavaScript runs is reported as exactly that. A check we could not read is shown as "not measured", never as a pass.

What if you cannot crawl my site?

If your firewall or host blocks CitedBot, the report says so and shows how to allow it. Change the setting and write to us, and we look at it with you. If it is not worth it to you, the 14-day money-back guarantee covers it, no reason needed.

Which flags can I ignore?

Every issue has a severity, so you start with the high ones. Checks that are optional say so: no major assistant is confirmed to read an llms.txt file, for example. A check we could not read is shown as "not measured", never as a failure.

Do you guarantee ChatGPT will mention my site?

No. Nobody controls what an assistant answers. The audit finds what blocks or weakens your pages for AI search and gives you the fix.

Can I cancel?

Yes, in your account, any time. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Inside 14 days of paying you can also ask for the full amount back.
