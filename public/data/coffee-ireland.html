What the named shops have in common

Ranked by strength of evidence. These are findings about 16 answers, not causes.

  • MeasuredTwo runs of the same question share on average 55% of the shops they link: count several runs rather than trusting one answer.
  • MeasuredLinks almost always go to the shop's own site: 60 of 84. Third-party sites (department stores, maps, review sites) account for 24 links: irelandbeforeyoudie.com (3), thegloss.ie (3), amazingfoodanddrink.com (2), coffeeroast.com (2), coffeeroasterfinder.com (2).
  • MeasuredClaude linked 1 domain with no working page (moo.coffee (1x), checked 18 Oct 2026): a buyer who clicks gets an error or an unrelated site. They are excluded from the table above.
  • Not tested yetFacts shown on the page (starting price, delivery time, free-shipping threshold, subscription terms), reviews and mentions in third-party guides: we have not yet compared them between named and unnamed shops. That is for the next measurement rounds.

Method and limits

  • Engine: Claude (Anthropic API, model claude-haiku-4-5), web search on, no history or personalisation. API answers differ from what a signed-in person sees in the app.
  • Questions: 8 buyer questions written in English, each asked 2 times in a fresh chat (16 answers kept, 19 Oct 2026).
  • “Linked”: the shop's domain is among the answer's links. We do not count a name in running text, and we never round “linked in 1 of 2 runs” up to “recommended”.
  • Limits: one engine and one model, answers change from day to day. No traffic or sales estimates. Domains are as Claude linked them; on 19 Oct 2026 we checked which ones load.
  • Raw data: every answer is kept with its timestamp; the table is downloadable as CSV.
