64 Claude answers to 32 buyer questions in 2 EU niches: who gets linked

What the numbers say

  • MeasuredLinking is concentrated. The most-linked shop appears in 31% to 56% of a cell's answers; the top three shops take 17% to 31% of shop links.
  • MeasuredA single answer is a poor guide. Two runs of the same question share only 45% to 77% of the shop sites they link, on average per cell.
  • MeasuredLinks go to shops' own sites: 279 of 326 links across the 4 cells (86%). Department stores, maps, guides and review sites take the rest.
  • MeasuredClaude linked 1 domain with no working page (confirmed by a second check), in 1 of the 4 cells, 1 time in total. It is excluded from the rankings.
  • Not tested yetWhy a shop gets linked: published facts (starting price, delivery time, subscription terms), reviews, third-party mentions or technical traits. That needs a linked-versus-unlinked comparison; it is the next measurement round.

Method and limits

  • Engine: Claude Haiku 4.5 (claude-haiku-4-5) through the Anthropic API, web search on (at most 2 searches per answer), no history or personalisation. API answers differ from what a signed-in person sees in the Claude app, and other assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) may link different shops: this study covers one engine only.
  • Questions: 32 buyer questions in French and Dutch (8 per cell), each asked twice, 64 calls in total. 64 complete answers kept; failed runs are dropped (none failed).
  • “Linked”: the shop's domain is among the answer's links. We do not count names in running text.
  • Coverage: 2 niches (loose-leaf tea and specialty coffee), 2 countries (Belgium and France), 2 languages. 64 answers is a small sample: read the percentages as counts, not as market share.
  • Limits: one engine, one model, runs on 18 Oct 2026; answers change from day to day. No traffic or sales estimates. Domains are as Claude linked them; we checked which ones load on 18 Oct 2026. Earlier counts from a different method (ChatGPT's app, 13 and 14 Oct) are retired and not mixed in here.
  • Raw data: every answer is stored with its timestamp. Pooled CSV: download.
