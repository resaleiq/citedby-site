128 Claude answers to 64 buyer questions in 5 EU niches: who gets linked

We asked Claude 64 buyer questions about beauty and collagen supplements, food supplements, loose-leaf tea, mattresses and specialty coffee in Belgium, France and Ireland, twice each (128 answers, 18 Oct 2026 to 19 Oct 2026). The most-linked shop appears in 19% to 56% of answers, and the top three take 17% to 32% of all shop links.

What the numbers say

  • MeasuredLinking is concentrated. The most-linked shop appears in 19% to 56% of a cell's answers; the top three shops take 17% to 32% of shop links.
  • MeasuredA single answer is a poor guide. Two runs of the same question share only 32% to 77% of the shop sites they link, on average per cell.
  • MeasuredLinks go to shops' own sites: 466 of 652 links across the 8 cells (71%). Department stores, maps, guides and review sites take the rest.
  • MeasuredClaude linked 4 domains with no working page (confirmed by a second check), in 3 of the 8 cells, 4 times in total. They are excluded from the rankings.
  • Not tested yetWhy a shop gets linked: published facts (starting price, delivery time, subscription terms), reviews, third-party mentions or technical traits. That needs a linked-versus-unlinked comparison; it is the next measurement round.

Method and limits

  • Engine: Claude Haiku 4.5 (claude-haiku-4-5) through the Anthropic API, web search on (at most 2 searches per answer), no history or personalisation. API answers differ from what a signed-in person sees in the Claude app, and other assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) may link different shops: this study covers one engine only.
  • Questions: 64 buyer questions in English, French and Dutch (8 per cell), each asked twice, 128 calls in total. 128 complete answers kept; failed runs are dropped (none failed).
  • “Linked”: the shop's domain is among the answer's links. We do not count names in running text.
  • Coverage: 5 niches (beauty and collagen supplements, food supplements, loose-leaf tea, mattresses and specialty coffee), 3 countries (Belgium, France and Ireland), 3 languages. 128 answers is a small sample: read the percentages as counts, not as market share.
  • Limits: one engine, one model, runs on 18 Oct 2026 to 19 Oct 2026; answers change from day to day. No traffic or sales estimates. Domains are as Claude linked them; we checked which ones load on 18 and 19 Oct 2026. Earlier counts from a different method (ChatGPT's app, 13 and 14 Oct) are retired and not mixed in here.
  • Raw data: every answer is stored with its timestamp. Pooled CSV: download.
