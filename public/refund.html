Refunds

Last updated: 21 October 2026

14-day right of withdrawal

You can withdraw from any purchase within 14 days of paying, as EU consumer law gives you, and you do not need a reason. Our money-back guarantee below goes further and refunds the full amount in every case, so you never pay for work already done.

14-day money-back guarantee

This covers the Single audit (€9), Starter, Pro and Agency, monthly or yearly. If it is not for you, write to us within 14 days of paying and we refund the full amount, even if you have already run audits.

Withdraw online

You can also withdraw online, any time: use the Withdraw from contract here function. It ends your plan: at once with a full refund if you paid in the last 14 days, otherwise at the end of the period you paid for. It takes two steps (a short form, then one confirmation button). We record your statement with the time it was made, email you an acknowledgement, and handle the refund within 14 days of your statement. If we cannot match your contract number to your email address automatically, a person checks it and writes to you within 2 working days.

Free trial on monthly plans

Monthly Starter, Pro and Agency start with 7 days free. You enter a card, nothing is charged today, and the checkout page shows the date and amount of the first charge before you confirm. We email you 3 days before that charge with a one-click cancel link. If you cancel before the first charge, you pay nothing. The 14-day money-back guarantee runs from the day of your first payment. One trial per email address and card. Yearly plans have no trial and are charged when you start.

Cancel a subscription

Starter, Pro and Agency renew until you cancel. Cancel from your account page ("Manage or cancel your plan"): enter your email, Stripe sends you a link, and the next charge stops. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You do not need to write to us. During a free trial, the link in our emails cancels in one click. Yearly plans are charged once a year and are cancelled the same way.

How to ask

Write to hello@citedby.eu with the site URL. We reply within 2 working days and refund to the original payment method through Stripe, within 14 days of your request.

Model withdrawal form

To withdraw from the contract, you can also send us this text by email (EU Directive 2011/83/EU, Annex I(B)). You do not have to use it.

To CitedBy, hello@citedby.eu:
I hereby give notice that I withdraw from my contract for the supply of the following service: [service ordered].
Ordered on [date]. Name of consumer: [name]. Address of consumer: [address, only if you want a postal reply]. Date: [date].
