About CitedBy

CitedBy (citedby.eu) is a website audit for AI search. You enter a URL, we crawl your site up to your plan's page limit, check whether AI search crawlers can reach and read it, and give you a graded report with the exact fix for each issue.

Which CitedBy is this

This is citedby.eu. It is not citedby.co or citedby.ai, which are different companies, and it is not an academic citation tool: it does not format, count or look up scholarly references. The only domain we use is citedby.eu.

What we do

  • Crawl your site from one URL, up to your plan's page limit, and score every page crawled for AI-crawler access, readable content, llms.txt, structured data, titles and headings, internal links and answer-first content.
  • Give the fix for each issue, copy-paste where possible.
  • Re-crawl on a schedule so you can see the score move; white-label PDF reports and multi-site monitoring for agencies.

What we do not do

We do not promise rankings or citations by any assistant, and we do not sell reviews, links or mentions. How each number is produced is on the methodology page.

How we earn money

From audits and plans, listed on the pricing page, with a 14-day money-back guarantee (refund terms). We take no commission from anything we recommend.

Editorial standards

  • Who writes. The guides, studies and reports on this site are written by the CitedBy editorial team, the people who run CitedBy. Articles carry the team byline, not an individual's name.
  • How claims are checked. Every statement about how a crawler or assistant behaves is checked against our own crawls and the data pages, or against a public document that the article cites. Each article lists its sources at the end.
  • What "Reviewed on" means. The date the team last re-read the article and re-checked its sources and numbers. A changed number gets a new date.
  • Corrections. If you find an error, write to hello@citedby.eu with the page address. We fix it and update the review date.
  • Method. How each number is produced is on the methodology page.
