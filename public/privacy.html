Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

Who is responsible

The controller for your personal data is the operator of CitedBy named in our legal notice (name and postal address). Write to hello@citedby.eu for any privacy question or request.

Cookies and logs

The public pages set no cookies and run no third-party scripts. We set a cookie only when you need it. Each one is strictly necessary, HttpOnly and never used for tracking or advertising: ci_session keeps you signed in for 30 days; ci_gstate lasts 10 minutes during "Continue with Google" to check that the sign-in you return from is the one you started; and opening a password-protected shared report sets one cookie for 24 hours so you do not retype the password. Our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, time) for security and to count visits, for 14 days. Our own counters record anonymous events per day and language (check started, check completed, email submitted, button clicked, offer viewed), with no IP address or identifier.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Audits, reports, crawled pages, projects, brands and Search Console data: until you delete them or your account. A single audit is available for 30 days.
  • Withdrawal records (/withdraw): 6 years, with the payment records; deleting your account does not remove them.
  • Sign-in and reset links: a link works once and for 15 minutes; we store only a hash. The record stays until you delete your account; for a signup you never confirmed there is no account, so write to hello@citedby.eu and we delete it.
  • Free-trial record (email address, its domain, card fingerprint): kept for as long as we offer a free trial, so that each person gets one; write to hello@citedby.eu to ask for deletion.
  • Free instant check (/t/): site address and a scrambled form of your IP address, 7 days.
  • Team invitations: until the person is removed from the team or the account is deleted.
  • Server access logs (with IP address): 14 days.
  • Mail log (address, subject and message text, with sign-in and reset links removed): until you delete your account.
  • Mini-audit requests: unconfirmed ones are deleted after 7 days, all of them after 12 months.
  • Invoices, payment records and Stripe's payment notifications: 6 years (Spanish Commercial Code art. 30). Deleting your account does not remove them.
  • Messages to hello@citedby.eu: they stay in the operator's mailbox; we delete a conversation when you ask.

Do you have to give us your data?

You need an email address to create an account, a site address to run an audit, and payment details to subscribe. Without them we cannot provide the service. Everything else is optional.

Your account and signing in

We keep your email address, the language you chose, a password hash if you set a password and, if you subscribe, the Stripe subscription id and plan. You can also sign in with Google ("Continue with Google"). We ask Google for your email address only (the openid and email scopes), and we accept it only if Google has verified it. Google sends us your email address and an account ID; we keep only the email address, and no name, photo, contacts or Google token. We store the email address like any other account address, and we create the account or sign you in with it. The legal basis is performing the contract with you, which is giving you access to your account (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Signing in with Google is optional; the email sign-in link works without it. We send you a sign-in link by email; it works once and for 15 minutes, and we store it only as a hash. We keep a mail log of what we send you, which holds the address, subject and message text, with sign-in and reset links removed. Stripe keeps payment and invoice records on its own side.

Site audits

When you run an audit we store your email address, the web address you audit, the public pages we fetched from it (address, title, headings, up to 6,000 characters of each page's text and the checks that passed or failed) and the report. We fetch only public pages, never log in, and identify ourselves as CitedBot. Reports stay until you delete your account, or earlier if you ask at hello@citedby.eu; a single audit is available for 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, its correction or deletion, a restriction of its use, and a copy in a portable format. You can also object to uses based on legitimate interest. Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect what was done before. You can export your account data from your dashboard and delete your account there. Deleting cancels any active subscription first, then removes your account, your sign-in links, your subscription records, the mail log entries for your address, your audits and the pages they crawled, your projects, brands and Search Console data, and your team invitations and memberships. It does not remove the free-trial record (email address, its domain and a card fingerprint from Stripe, kept so each person gets one trial), Stripe's payment notifications and invoices (kept 6 years under Spanish Commercial Code art. 30), or a withdrawal you sent us (see Withdrawal records above). To ask for anything else to be removed, write to hello@citedby.eu. For anything else, write to the same address; we answer within one month.

Complaints

If you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es), or to the authority in your own EU country. We would rather hear from you first at hello@citedby.eu.

Automated decisions

We make no decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. Audit scores describe web pages, not people.
