Privacy

The public pages set no cookies and run no third-party scripts. One cookie exists: if you sign in to an account, we set ci_session. It keeps you signed in for 30 days, is strictly necessary for that, is HttpOnly, and is never used for tracking or advertising. Our server keeps standard access logs (IP address, page, time) for security and to count visits; they are kept only as long as needed for those purposes.

If you email us or buy the audit, we use your shop URL and email address only to deliver the audit and answer you. Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles card details; we never see them. Our own counters record anonymous events per day and language (check started, check completed, email submitted, Fix Pack link clicked), with no IP address or identifier. If you ask for the email mini-audit, we store your email address, shop address, country, language and niche, and send a confirmation email first (double opt-in). We write to you only about that audit and one follow-up, delete unconfirmed requests after 7 days and all requests after 12 months, and you can delete yours at any time with the unsubscribe link in our emails. Our server logs your IP address briefly to limit abuse. To access or delete your data, write to hello@citedby.eu.

If you save a count to an account, we keep your email address, the language you chose, your shop name and address, the saved counts (which assistants named your shop, which rivals they named) and, if you subscribe, the Stripe subscription id and plan. We send you a sign-in link by email; it works once and for 15 minutes, and we store it only as a hash. If you choose Continue with Google instead, Google tells us your email address and that it has verified it; we ask for nothing else and store nothing else from Google. We keep a mail log of what we send you, which holds the address and subject but not the sign-in link. Stripe keeps payment and invoice records on its own side.

Site audits

When you run an audit we store your email address, the web address you audit, the public pages we fetched from it (address, title, headings, a short text excerpt and the checks that passed or failed) and the report. We fetch only public pages, never log in, and identify ourselves as CitedBot. Reports stay in your account until you delete the audit or the account; a single audit is kept for 30 days of access.

Who processes your data

Recipients of personal data (GDPR Art. 13(1)(e)-(f)): Stripe Payments Europe Ltd (Ireland; payments and subscriptions, card details never reach us; it may process data in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses). Resend (United States; sends our emails, so it receives your email address, the subject and the message text, including sign-in links, receipts, reports and mini-audit emails; Standard Contractual Clauses). Anthropic, PBC (United States; Standard Contractual Clauses), which receives the public text of pages we crawled, to write the fixes, and the buyer-style questions we ask it, built from the product category you type plus a country and language; it does not receive your email address or account data. OpenRouter (United States; Standard Contractual Clauses), which receives the same buyer-style questions, and not your email address or account data. PostHog (EU cloud, Frankfurt, operated by PostHog Inc., United States; Standard Contractual Clauses), which counts page visits: our server passes it your IP address and browser type with each page view so it can tell visits apart for one day; it sets no cookie, stores neither the address nor the browser type, and is not used when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Forward Email (Forward Email LLC, United States; Standard Contractual Clauses), which receives each message sent to hello@citedby.eu (sender address and message) and forwards it to the operator's Gmail inbox, provided by Google Ireland Ltd (EU), which may transfer data to Google LLC (United States) under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Hetzner Online GmbH (our servers, in Helsinki, Finland, EU), a German company, which hosts our site and database. Google (Google Ireland Ltd, EU, which may transfer data to Google LLC, United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework) also runs the sign-in if you choose Continue with Google: it knows that you signed in to CitedBy. Slack (Slack Technologies Limited, Ireland; Salesforce, Inc., United States; Standard Contractual Clauses) receives our project alerts (the audited site's address, its score, how many issues are new or fixed and what got worse, plus a link to the report) only at a Slack webhook address you give us in your project settings; with none set, nothing goes to Slack. Cloudflare, Inc. (United States; Standard Contractual Clauses) answers the public DNS lookups we make for the site address you audit, so it receives that site address and our server's IP address, and no account data. Each processes data only for its function. We do not sell data.

You can export your account data from your dashboard and delete your account there. Deleting cancels any active subscription first, then removes the account, shop, saved counts and recount reports, sign-in links, and your address from the mail log. The audit also stores the site address you enter and the pages it crawled; write to hello@citedby.eu to have those removed.
