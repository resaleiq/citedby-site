AI visibility tools compared by what they cost and do

Updated 18 October 2026. Most tools in this category are dashboards: you pay a monthly fee and they track whether assistants mention your brand. CitedBy differs on one point: we also make the fixes. Check each vendor's own pricing page, because prices change.

  • Our prices are public on the home page. Refund conditions are on the refund page.

How the other tools are positioned

  • Profound: aimed at larger brands and enterprise teams. On 18 October 2026 its own site showed no public price.

Which to pick
