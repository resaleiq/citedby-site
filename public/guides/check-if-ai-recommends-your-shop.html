How to check whether AI assistants recommend your shop

By the CitedBy editorial team · Reviewed on 2026-10-20

Do it by hand in 10 minutes

  1. Write 5 questions a buyer would type, for your product and country. Example: "best online shop for specialty coffee in Belgium". Do not put your shop name in them.
  2. Ask each question in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini, logged out, with web search on.
  3. Ask each question a second time. Answers change between runs.
  4. For each answer note two things: is your shop named in the text, and is your domain in the links.
  5. Add it up: named in 3 of 40 answers is a different situation from 0 of 40.

Check you are reading the right company

Ask each assistant "what is [your shop name]" first. If it describes another business with a similar name, fix that before anything else: add an About page with your legal name, country and domain, put Organization data in JSON-LD on the home page, and link the same facts from your listings.

If you are not named

The fixes that tend to matter are on your own site (a page that answers the buyer question directly, product data with price and availability) and off it (the pages assistants cite for your category, such as comparison articles and directories). Questions people ask

How many questions should I ask?

Five questions asked twice in three assistants gives 30 answers, enough to see whether you are named never, sometimes or often. More questions give a steadier picture.

Why ask each question twice?

Answers change between runs, so one answer can mislead. Log both and count them separately.

Does the audit tell me if assistants name my shop?

No. It checks whether your pages can be read and quoted. The manual method above shows whether you are named today.
