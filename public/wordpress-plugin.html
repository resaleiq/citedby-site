CitedBy AI-Search Check for WordPress

A free WordPress plugin. Open Tools, AI-Search Check, press one button, and see whether AI-search crawlers can read your site: your robots.txt for ten named crawlers, your llms.txt, your sitemap, and a page check of your home page run from citedby.eu. It reports; it changes nothing on your site.

Download the plugin (zip, version 0.1.1) Try it in your browser

Install

  1. In wp-admin open Plugins, Add New Plugin, Upload Plugin, and choose the zip.
  2. Activate it.
  3. Open Tools, AI-Search Check, and press "Run the check".

It needs WordPress 5.8 or newer and PHP 7.4 or newer. It is licensed GPL v2 or later. Until it is in the WordPress plugin directory, install it from the zip.

What it checks

  • Whether Settings, Reading has "Discourage search engines" ticked.
  • Whether robots.txt closes the whole site to OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot or Bingbot. Blocking a training-only crawler is shown as a normal choice, not as a fix.
  • Whether you have a sitemap and an llms.txt.
  • From outside, by CitedBy: whether your home page text is in the HTML a crawler receives, and whether it carries Organization structured data.

It cannot see a firewall or CDN rule that turns a crawler away. The page check from citedby.eu shows what an outside visitor receives.

What it sends

  • Nothing until an administrator presses the button. No front-end code, no cookies, nothing stored in your database.
  • On each press: your home page address and your admin language go to citedby.eu, which loads the page and sends the result back. Your server's address is seen by any web service; it is used to rate-limit and is not stored with your site address.
  • The "full audit" button is a normal link to our audit. Nothing is sent unless you click it.
