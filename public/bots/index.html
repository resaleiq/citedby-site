AI crawlers: who they are, what they do, how to allow or block them

One page per crawler, with the operator, the robots.txt token, the operator's own sentence, what blocking costs and how to verify a request, each read from the operator's own page on the date shown.

An AI crawler is a bot that fetches pages for an assistant such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Gemini, for a search index or for model training. Each page names the operator, the robots.txt token, what blocking changes and how to verify a request, so you can decide per bot whether to allow it.

Copy-paste robots.txt: opt out of model training, stay in AI search

Operators that say their crawler may be used to train models: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent, Amazonbot. The first block turns those off. The second keeps the search and answer crawlers on: OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Amzn-SearchBot, Meta-WebIndexer. This is a choice for your business, and a Disallow only covers well-behaved bots.
