What this page compares

All three tools track how AI assistants answer a set of prompts: whether your brand is mentioned, which sources are cited, how that changes over time. This page lists only what each vendor publishes on its own pricing page, with the date we read it. It makes no claim about which tool is better, because the pricing pages cannot show that. Prices are for monthly billing. Where a vendor publishes no figure, the cell says so.

What the lowest listed monthly price is

The lowest listed monthly price in the table is OtterlyAI Lite at €29 a month, for 15 search prompts. The lowest Peec AI price is €85 a month for 50 prompts. Profound lists no monthly price. That is arithmetic from the table, not a verdict on value.

Questions people ask

Which of the three is cheapest?

By the lowest listed monthly price, OtterlyAI Lite at €29 a month. Profound lists no monthly price and Peec AI's entry plan is €85 a month. Plans differ in prompts, engines and limits, so the number alone does not decide it.

Does Profound have a public price?

Its pricing page lists a free Trial and an Enterprise plan with a custom price. We found no monthly price for a paid plan on that page on 19 October 2026.

Do Peec AI and OtterlyAI offer a discount for paying yearly?

Peec AI's FAQ says 15% for annual billing. OtterlyAI's pricing toggle shows 15% off for annual billing.

Does CitedBy track what ChatGPT says about my brand?

No. CitedBy checks whether AI crawlers can read your pages and gives you the fix. It does not track prompts or brand mentions in AI answers.
