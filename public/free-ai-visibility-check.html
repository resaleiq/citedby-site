Questions about the free check

Is the AI visibility check really free?

Yes. You need no account, no card and no email. We crawl up to 10 pages of your site and show the result on a page you can open straight away. Each network can run 3 free checks a day.

What does the free check look at?

It reads up to 10 public pages and checks the HTML each one serves, robots.txt, your sitemap and whether the crawlers of ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can get in. It shows your score, your AI crawler access and the 3 issues to fix first.

Does it ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity about my business?

No. It does not query any assistant. It checks what stops those assistants from reading your pages. For Google's crawlers, which Gemini relies on, use the AI crawler checker, which tests Google-Extended and Googlebot too.

Will fixing the issues make ChatGPT mention my site?

We cannot promise that, and nobody can: assistants decide what to name. The check finds what blocks or weakens your pages for AI search and gives you the fix. That is the part you control.

What is the difference between the free check and the paid audit?

The free check reads 10 pages and shows the score, crawler access and 3 issues. The audit reads up to your plan's page limit, groups repeated problems into single fixes and writes the exact code for each one. From €9, once, with a 14-day money-back guarantee.
